Hermes

Hermes is a personal-use command-line client that connects to Google Workspace APIs on behalf of its single user, who is also its developer.

What it does

Hermes is an assistant application that runs locally on the developer's own computer. When the developer asks it to, it reads and writes their own Google data: calendar events, Drive files, Sheets, Docs, Gmail messages and contacts. It acts only on the account its owner explicitly authorises, and only when that owner issues a request in the local application.

Who can use it

One person: its developer, using their own Google Account. Hermes is not distributed, published, sold, or offered as a service to any third party. There is no sign-up, no public endpoint, and no account other than the developer's.

Where data goes

Google API responses are processed on the developer's machine and stored there. Hermes runs no server of its own and transmits no Google user data to any third party. Credentials are kept in files readable only by the local user.
